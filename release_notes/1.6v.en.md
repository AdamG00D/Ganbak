| `Wasl-1.6-arm64-v8a.apk` | **Most phones** (2017 and newer) — pick this if unsure |
| `Wasl-1.6-armeabi-v7a.apk` | Older or low-end phones |

All variants share the same version code, so you can update from any of them to any other.
Android 7.0+. Verify with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. Website: https://adamg00d.github.io/Wasl/

## What's new

- **More privacy — relays can't tell who talks to whom:**
  - Private chats travel with secret tags that change every 10 minutes instead of names, and every message has a
    fixed size.
  - Messages that wait for their owner no longer show sender or recipient — only the recipient can tell it's theirs.
    By default you only accept them from people you've talked to before (Settings → Privacy).
  - In private groups members stay hidden — only the owner and admins show up.
- **Secret groups**: nobody sees them at all; people join only by an invite from the owner or an admin.
- **Group invites**: Group info → *Invite someone*. Your friend joins with one tap, no password. In join-request groups
  the invite goes to the owner for approval (unless the owner invited).
- **Who's the spy, cheat-proof**: all phones deal the roles and the word together — nobody knows the spy except the
  spy, not even whoever opened the game — and at the end every phone checks that nobody cheated.
- **Mafia**: the roles are sealed with a fingerprint at the start, and every phone checks at the end that they didn't
  change.
