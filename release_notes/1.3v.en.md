## Download

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Wasl-1.3-arm64-v8a.apk` | **Most phones** (2017 and newer) — pick this if unsure |
| `Wasl-1.3-armeabi-v7a.apk` | Older or low-end phones |
| `Wasl-1.3-x86_64.apk` | Emulators and Chromebooks |
| `Wasl-1.3-universal.apk` | Any device (larger download) |

All variants share the same version code, so you can update from any of them to any other.
Android 7.0+. Verify with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new

- **Games inside chats** 🎮 — tap the games button next to the text box.
  - With a friend: tic-tac-toe, four in a row, battleships, rock paper scissors, dots and boxes, memory.
  - With a group: who's the spy?, quiz (questions in Arabic and English) and mafia, plus dots and boxes and memory.

  The invite lands in the chat as a card: your friend taps *Play*; in a group whoever wants to play taps *Join* and
  whoever opened it taps *Start*. The card keeps the score afterwards; there's a rematch button and quick reactions
  inside the game.
- **A help page for every game** (the ? button): goal, steps, roles, tips and FAQ.
- **No cheating** at rock paper scissors or battleships, not even by whoever opened the game: picks and fleets are
  locked with a fingerprint and revealed at the end, and the app names anyone who lied.
- **A new look, "Khayamiya"** — saffron and night ink instead of the generic purple, stitched lines for connections,
  a khayamiya pattern on the connection and game cards, and Ramadan bunting when someone wins. New launcher icon.
  The old colours are still in *Settings → App color*.
- **"Around you now"** strip above the chats shows who is nearby.
- **Wasl guide** in Settings (and a ? on the first screen): every way to connect (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi / hotspot, both)
  explained with a drawing — when to use it, its limits and how to start — plus groups, games and safety.

## Fixes

- Every screen was checked with large text (like the phone's font-size setting); nothing spills over any more.

## Notes

- Games need both sides on 1.3. Older versions ignore game messages without breaking, and normal chat between 1.3
  and older versions works as before.
- In mafia, who's the spy and quiz the phone that opened the game is the referee (it deals roles and counts votes) —
  see SECURITY.md.
