## Download

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Wasl-1.4-arm64-v8a.apk` | **Most phones** (2017 and newer) — pick this if unsure |
| `Wasl-1.4-armeabi-v7a.apk` | Older or low-end phones |
| `Wasl-1.4-x86_64.apk` | Emulators and Chromebooks |
| `Wasl-1.4-universal.apk` | Any device (larger download) |

All variants share the same version code, so you can update from any of them to any other.
Android 7.0+. Verify with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new

- **Messages wait for their owner** — if the person you're writing to isn't around, just type. The message is sealed
  for them alone, and your phone and the phones around you carry it for up to 48 hours and hand it over as soon as
  they show up; carriers can't read it. You see *Delivered* when it reaches them. (Text only, with people you've
  talked to before.)
- **SOS** — under *Nearby* tap *SOS* and write where you are or what you need; everyone running Wasl in the mesh gets
  an alert until you stop it.
- **Share Wasl** (in Settings) — your friend doesn't have the app? Your phone opens a network and they scan two codes:
  the first joins the network (with the name and password underneath for cameras that can't read Wi-Fi codes), the
  second opens a page where they download Wasl straight from your phone — no internet needed.
- **Wasl network**:
  - A *Network QR* button so any phone can join with its camera.
  - When many people around you are on Bluetooth only, one of them is offered to start a Wasl network so everyone
    gets faster.

## Fixes

- Libraries the app uses were updated (permissions, saving photos and others).

## Notes

- Offline messages and SOS need both sides on 1.4. Older versions ignore them without breaking.
- The *Share Wasl* page only works while that screen is open and serves only the page and the app. Compare the file
  fingerprint on the page with the one on your screen. See SECURITY.md.
- SOS: names in Wasl aren't verified, so whoever sees one should check before acting.
