## Wasl is now called Ganbak (جنبك) 🎉

Same app, same features, a new name that says what it does: talk to the people next to you (*ganbak* = "next to
you") with no internet.

## ⚠️ If you have Wasl installed

- Ganbak is a new app on your phone (the package name changed), so it won't update Wasl: install Ganbak, then remove
  Wasl.
- Chats saved in Wasl don't move to Ganbak.
- Ganbak talks to friends still on Wasl 1.6, so nobody gets cut off while everyone switches.

## What's new

- New name, new website: https://adamg00d.github.io/Ganbak/

Everything from Wasl 1.6 is here: encrypted chats where relays can't tell who talks to whom, groups (including secret
ones), invites, messages that wait for their owner, SOS, games (including a cheat-proof Who's the spy), Ganbak network
and Share Ganbak.
