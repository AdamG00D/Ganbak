All variants share the same version code, so you can update from any of them to any other.
Android 7.0+. Verify with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. Website: https://adamg00d.github.io/Wasl/

This release includes everything new since 1.2.

## ⚠️ Before you update

- Wasl 1.5 uses a new, signed identity, so it **can't talk to older versions** — ask everyone you talk to to update.
- Old saved chats stay readable but won't reconnect to the same person; start a new chat from *Nearby*.
- Old groups: create them again once everyone has updated.

## What's new

- **Nobody can speak for you** — your name, picture and group messages are signed with your key, so nobody can change
  them on the way or post in a group as you.
  - Everyone has a **fingerprint** (a small khayamiya pattern from their key): chat menu → *About*.
  - When two people with the same name are around, a small pattern next to each tells them apart.
  - If someone shows up with the name of a person you talked to before but isn't them, Wasl says **"Not them?"** and
    warns you inside the chat.
  - Give anyone a name of your own.
- **Messages wait for their owner** — if the person isn't around, just type. The message is sealed for them alone, and
  phones around you carry it for up to 48 hours and hand it over as soon as they show up; carriers can't read it.
- **SOS** — under *Nearby* tap *SOS* and write where you are or what you need; everyone running Wasl in the mesh gets
  an alert until you stop it.
- **Share Wasl** (Settings) — your friend doesn't have the app? Your phone opens a network and they scan two codes:
  the first joins the network, the second opens a page where they download Wasl straight from your phone — no internet.
- **Wasl network** — no router? Under *Nearby* tap *Start a Wasl network* and your phone makes its own Wi-Fi (no
  internet); people next to you join with one tap or a QR code — much faster than Bluetooth, with video and calls.
  When many people around are on Bluetooth only, one of them is offered to start one.
- **Games inside chats** 🎮 — tic-tac-toe, four in a row, battleships, rock paper scissors, dots and boxes and memory
  with a friend; who's the spy?, quiz and mafia with a group. Every game has a help page, and nobody can cheat.
- **A new look, "Khayamiya"**, a new icon, and the **Wasl guide** in Settings explaining every way to connect.
- **"Around you now"** strip above the chats.

## Fixes

- Every screen was checked with large text; nothing spills over.
- Wi-Fi notices by itself when the phone joins a new network.
- Libraries updated.

## Notes

- Names in Wasl are free and can repeat — Wasl tells people apart by key. The sure way to verify someone is the
  safety code, face to face.
- A Wasl network shares its name and password with people nearby running Wasl so they can join; private chats stay
  end-to-end encrypted.
- The *Share Wasl* page only works while that screen is open and serves only the page and the app.
- Details in SECURITY.md.
